Product Analyst (Fresher) - Bengaluru

About the role
Help our product managers decide what to build next. You'll own metrics, run experiments and tell the story behind the numbers.

What you'll do
- Define and track product metrics such as activation, retention and conversion
- Analyse funnels and cohorts in SQL and our product analytics tools
- Design and read A/B tests
- Write clear recommendations for product managers and leadership

Must have
- Strong SQL
- Experience with product analytics tools such as Mixpanel or Amplitude
- A/B testing and basic statistics
- Excel or Google Sheets
- Clear written and spoken communication

Nice to have
- Python or R for analysis
- Dashboards in Looker, Tableau or Power BI
- Case competition or consulting experience
- Degree in Economics, Statistics, Engineering or a related field
