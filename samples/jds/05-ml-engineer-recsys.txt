Machine Learning Engineer, Recommendations (1-3 years) - Hyderabad

About the role
Our Recommendations team trains and serves the models behind the home feed for 20M users. You'll own models from training data to online A/B tests.

What you'll do
- Train and tune ranking and recommendation models in PyTorch
- Build feature pipelines in Spark and SQL over billions of events
- Run offline evaluation (NDCG, recall@k) and online A/B tests
- Deploy models with Kubernetes and monitor drift in production
- Work with product managers to turn metrics into roadmap decisions

Must have
- 1+ years training deep learning models with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Strong statistics: hypothesis testing, A/B test design
- Spark or another distributed data processing framework
- Strong SQL and Python
- Degree in Computer Science, Statistics or Mathematics

Nice to have
- Experience with recommender systems or learning-to-rank
- MLflow or Kubeflow for experiment tracking
- Kubernetes and Docker
- Published papers or Kaggle competition results
- Feature stores such as Feast
